1. Executive Summary & Problem Definition

Over 1.4 billion citizens in India face severe barriers accessing legal justice and government welfare due to archaic legal jargon, low awareness of newly enacted criminal laws (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 replacing IPC 1860), language barriers across 22 scheduled languages, and high rates of AI hallucination when asking general LLMs for statutory advice.

NyayaNexus solves this by introducing a humanized, modular MVC system paired with an engineered 4-layer prompt grounding pipeline that eliminates fictional statute hallucinations, automatically maps legacy IPC sections to BNS 2023, guides citizens to legitimate government welfare schemes, and generates legally compliant procedural drafts (RTI Section 6(1), Zero FIR complaints, and Consumer demand notices).

2. System Architecture & Tech Stack

NyayaNexus strictly follows the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architectural paradigm to guarantee modularity and maintainability:

  • Model Layer: promptModel.js, legalModel.js, schemeModel.js, draftModel.js, chatSessionModel.js. Manages the verified statutory corpus, active prompt guardrails, and welfare schemes.
  • Controller Layer: chatController.js, schemeController.js, legalController.js, draftController.js, promptStudioController.js, teamController.js. Handles request orchestration and business logic.
  • Service Layer: promptEngine.js (multilingual normalization and grounding assembly), geminiService.js (Google Generative AI execution with temperature tuning at 0.25), legalGroundingService.js (zero-offline fallback engine).
  • View Layer: Server-side rendered EJS templates paired with responsive mobile-first modular CSS (variables.css, style.css, chat.css, schemes.css, studio.css).

3. Working Modules Overview

  1. Multilingual Indic AI Chatbot: Understands English, Hindi, and regional Indic languages via text and Web Speech API voice input.
  2. Grounded Prompt Engineering & Anti-Hallucination Pipeline: Dynamic injection of verified legal grounding with automatic Bar Council Rule 36 disclaimer enforcement.
  3. Government Welfare Scheme Eligibility Checker: Interactive parameter matching across central and state welfare schemes (PM-Kisan, Ayushman Bharat 70+, PMAY, MUDRA).
  4. BNS 2023 vs IPC 1860 Transition Explorer: Instant cross-statutory comparative lookup for criminal laws.
  5. Formal Legal Draft Generator: Formats downloadable, ready-to-file legal documents (RTI Section 6(1), Consumer Protection Demand Notice, BNSS 173 Zero FIR Complaint, and NI Act Section 138 Notice).
  6. Prompt Engineering Studio & Comparative Benchmarks: Live side-by-side inspection of naive/raw LLM prompts versus NyayaNexus grounded prompts.
  7. Emergency Legal & Cyber Helpline Directory: Real-time triaging for Cyber Fraud 1930 (Golden Hour protocol), NALSA Free Legal Aid 15100, and Emergency 112.

6. Limitations & Future Scope

  • Informational Nature: Adheres strictly to Bar Council of India Rule 36; provides legal awareness and empowerment rather than formal attorney-client advocacy.
  • State-Level Statutory Variations: While central laws (BNS, BNSS, RTI, Consumer Act) are fully grounded, certain localized municipal bylaws require continuous dataset expansion.
  • Future Scope: Integration of automated voice-in/voice-out pipelines for non-literate rural citizens and direct WhatsApp Bot integration.
